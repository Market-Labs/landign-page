# User Stories

## US-001: View Product Value Proposition

**As a** visitor,

**I want to** understand what MarketGo offers and the problem it solves,

**So that** I can determine whether the solution meets my needs.


### Acceptance Criteria:

**Scenario 1: Visitor accesses the landing page**

**Given** that the visitor accesses the MarketGo landing page,

**When** the page finishes loading,

**Then** the system displays a clear headline, a description of the product, and its main value proposition.

**Scenario 2: Visitor wants to learn more**

**Given** that the visitor is viewing the value proposition,

**When** the visitor reviews the main section,

**Then** the system displays a clear call-to-action that allows the visitor to continue exploring the solution.


---

## US-002: Explore Services and Benefits

**As a** visitor,

**I want to** review the main services, features, and benefits offered by MarketGo,

**So that** I can understand how the solution can help me.


### Acceptance Criteria:

**Scenario 1: Services are available**

**Given** that MarketGo has available services or features,

**When** the visitor accesses the services section,

**Then** the system displays the main services with their corresponding descriptions and benefits.

**Scenario 2: Visitor reviews a service**

**Given** that the visitor is viewing the available services,

**When** the visitor reviews a service,

**Then** the system displays enough information to understand what the service offers and its main benefit.


---

## US-003: Review Plans

**As a** visitor,

**I want to** review the available MarketGo plans, their features, and their pricing,

**So that** I can identify the plan that best matches my needs.


### Acceptance Criteria:

**Scenario 1: Plans are available**

**Given** that MarketGo has available plans,

**When** the visitor accesses the plans section,

**Then** the system displays the available plans with their names, prices, features, and corresponding call-to-action.

**Scenario 2: Visitor compares plans**

**Given** that multiple plans are available,

**When** the visitor reviews the plans,

**Then** the system displays their features and pricing in a consistent format that allows the visitor to compare them.


---

## US-004: Contact the Team

**As a** potential customer,

**I want to** send my contact information and a message to the MarketGo team,

**So that** I can request more information or receive assistance.


### Acceptance Criteria:

**Scenario 1: Visitor submits valid information**

**Given** that the visitor has entered all required contact information,

**When** the visitor submits the contact form,

**Then** the system processes the request and displays a confirmation message indicating that the message was sent successfully.

**Scenario 2: Required information is missing**

**Given** that the visitor has not completed one or more required fields,

**When** the visitor submits the contact form,

**Then** the system displays validation messages indicating which fields need to be completed.
